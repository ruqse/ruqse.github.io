* Vaginal VirBench

bioRxiv 2026

Benchmarking virus identification tools on vaginal metagenomes

  • Tests 14 tools across five evaluations: controlled genome fragments, simulated viral spike-ins, real assemblies, RCA-enriched viromes, and an independent cohort
  • Built on 13 assemblies from South African adolescents; the tool ranking held in an independent 30-sample Swedish cohort, MiTCH (Spearman ρ = 0.952)
  • geNomad and VIBRANT led with opposite errors: VIBRANT made 8 false viral calls and found 22% of viral contigs; geNomad made 201 and found 33%
PythonNextflowGPUcontainers
→ repo → preprint

* MiTCH: Multi-Omics of Bacterial Vaginosis

npj Women's Health 2026

How vaginal microbes and metabolites relate to bacterial vaginosis (co-first author)

  • Integrates shotgun metagenomics and targeted LC-MS metabolomics from 111 Swedish women (29 with BV, 82 controls) using DIABLO and MOFA2
  • Builds a Gardnerella pangenome from 61 metagenome-assembled genomes and links genes to metabolites
  • Analysis code, processed data, figures and supplementary tables are public; reads on ENA (PRJEB108308)
RDIABLOMOFA2MetaPhlAn 4LC-MS
→ repo → paper

* MetaPhlAn 4 vs VIRGO2 for Gardnerella

analysis

How two reference databases represent Gardnerella species

  • MetaPhlAn 4 co-bins G. piotii with G. pickettii and G. swidsinskii with G. leopoldii; its G. vaginalis row sums seven GTDB species clusters
  • VIRGO2 labels piotii, swidsinskii and leopoldii separately
  • A database-content comparison, not a read-level accuracy test; reproduces in about a minute from precomputed outputs
PythonMetaPhlAn 4VIRGO2GTDB

* Parascaris β-Tubulin & Benzimidazole Resistance

submitted

How β-tubulin genes differ between resistant and susceptible P. univalens

  • Sequences all seven β-tubulin genes as long PacBio HiFi amplicons from pooled eggs of a resistant and a susceptible farm, resolving 243 haplotypes
  • Nextflow DSL2 pipeline from HiFi reads to haplotypes, variants and identity matrices (pbaa, pbmm2, FreeBayes, bcftools)
  • R code regenerates every figure and table, then checks them against the numbers in the manuscript; reads on ENA (PRJEB102621)
Nextflow DSL2PacBio HiFiFreeBayesR
→ repo → Zenodo

* Gene Co-Expression Networks in P. univalens

PLOS ONE 2024

Tissue-specific response to ivermectin

  • RNA-seq of the anterior end and intestine of 28 adult worms exposed to ivermectin in vitro
  • Consensus network from 10 inference methods in Seidr, with Leiden module detection
  • Found seven modules associated with ivermectin response and 219 core genes
RSeidrLeidenDESeq2
